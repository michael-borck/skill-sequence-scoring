**One honest note on the proper scoring rules angle:**

The Gneiting & Raftery proper scoring rules literature is technically about probabilistic forecast evaluation rather than athletic scoring systems. The connection to your paper is conceptual — the idea that a scoring rule should elicit and reward the behaviour you actually care about — rather than a direct application. Worth citing as theoretical framing but don't over-claim the connection. One sentence in the Discussion is right, not a whole section.
