# Draft Email to David McCarthy

**To:** Find contact via institution or ResearchGate. The paper lists no email but was published while he was likely at a university. Try ResearchGate, LinkedIn, or Google Scholar author profile.

**Subject:** PBA bowling data request — follow-up research on scoring systems

---

Dear Dr McCarthy,

I am writing with reference to your 2011 CHANCE article "Estimating Different Sources of Variation and Predicting Tournament Outcomes in Professional Bowling," which I cite in my current research comparing traditional and World Bowling scoring systems.

Your dataset of 278,579 PBA National Tour games (2004–2010) would be invaluable for validating our simulation models. My paper, currently under review at the Journal of Quantitative Analysis in Sports, uses Monte Carlo simulation calibrated against published PBA statistics to compare score discrimination under traditional and current-frame scoring. Access to your game-level data would allow us to:

1. Validate our skill tier calibration against actual PBA ability distributions
2. Re-score real PBA games under both systems to test how often the winner would change
3. Extend the analysis to a Win Probability Added framework in a planned follow-up study

I recognise the data was collected over a decade ago and may no longer be readily available. If you do still have it, I would be grateful for access. I am happy to acknowledge the data source in all publications and share our results.

All code and analysis tools are publicly available at:
https://github.com/michael-borck/skill-sequence-scoring

Thank you for your time and for the excellent foundational work.

Best regards,
Michael Borck
School of Marketing and Management
Curtin University, Perth, Australia
michael.borck@curtin.edu.au
